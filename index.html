Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Bird ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Flappy Bird does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, or precise location.

The game may store certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as your score, best score, or game preferences. This information is used only to provide the game's functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Any information stored locally by the App is used only to operate and improve the gameplay experience, including saving scores, settings, and game progress.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Storage

Gameplay information stored by the App remains on your device unless otherwise stated in a future version of the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not intentionally collect personal information through third-party services.

If third-party services such as advertising, analytics, or other services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their data practices.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Since the App does not require users to provide personal information, no personal account information is maintained by us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
